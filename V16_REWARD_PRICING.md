# v16 Reward Pricing

- Link reward quotes now cost **3 points per $1** of detected or entered product price.
- Quotes round up to the next whole point.
- Minimum quote remains **1 point**.
- Maximum reward cost remains **350 points**.
- Examples: $10 = 30 points, $25 = 75 points, $50 = 150 points, $100 = 300 points.
- Existing private-link behavior, parent review, chore trading, rotation rules, theme, and stored family data are unchanged.
