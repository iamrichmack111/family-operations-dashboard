# v11 · Link Rewards + Chore Trade

## Link Rewards Store
- Paste a normal HTTP/HTTPS product link.
- The server attempts to read structured product-price metadata and the page title.
- Cost formula: **1 point per $1**, rounded up, minimum 1 point, maximum **350 points**.
- If a retailer blocks the scanner, enter the displayed price as a fallback and the app calculates the same point cost.
- The child sees the quote before requesting the item.
- Points are deducted into the request immediately; parent denial refunds them automatically.
- Parent fulfillment keeps the points spent.
- Private/local-network URLs are rejected by the scanner.

## Chore Trade Exchange
- Household members with chores can offer points for another member to take an assigned chore.
- Offers can be open to anyone or targeted to one member.
- Offered points are deducted into escrow when the trade is posted.
- Accepted trades instantly reassign the work and transfer escrowed points to the person accepting.
- Canceled, declined, stale, or invalidated trades refund the offerer.
- Basement + Laundry move together.
- Bathrooms + Kitchen Deep Clean move together.
- Trade history is visible in Household HQ and included in exports.

Existing family records, chore scheduling rules, optional photos, accounts, and PIN hashes are preserved.
